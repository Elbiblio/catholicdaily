- Stable ID: `raymond_of_penyafort`

- Feast date and calendar scope: January 7; optional memorial in the General Roman Calendar
- Identity conflicts resolved: The reviewed sources place Raymond's birth variously around 1175 or around 1180, so the profile uses `c. 1180` rather than asserting an exact year. The famous sea-cloak episode and precise miracle claims were not established by the historical controls and are omitted.

## Content review

The content review checked the stable and celebration IDs, optional-memorial date, approximate birth, 1275 death, Bologna formation, 1222 Dominican entry, penitential writing, Liber Extra commission, and 1238–1240 leadership against the specialist and Catholic sources. It rejects the corrupt legacy biography fragment, avoids the unsupported claim that Raymond created the first canon-law code, and names the morally mixed effects of his medieval public influence. Every life section and virtue has evidence links, and the practices concern careful judgment and accessible justice rather than generic productivity.

## Theological review

The theological review keeps Jesus Christ and the salvation of persons above legal technique. It presents law as accountable to truth, mercy, rights, duties, and the common good, without suggesting that pastoral care permits arbitrary disregard of norms. It neither canonizes discriminatory medieval policy nor treats holiness as proof that every historical decision was good. Prayer asks for conversion and just service through Christ rather than success, prestige, or legal power.
