## Content review

The profile preserves manuscript and institutional history, labels reported experiences, centers active mercy, and excludes magic, guaranteed outcomes, numerical compulsion, retrospective diagnosis, medical neglect, unsafe penance, and harmful obedience. Summary is 100–150 words.

## Theological review

Private revelation remains subordinate to Christ and the deposit of faith. Mercy includes truth, justice, consent, boundaries, safeguarding, and professional care; it cannot silence victims or force reconciliation. Spiritual direction never authorizes harm or concealment of abuse.
