## Uncertainties and conflicts

- Augustine's birth, age, and early monastic biography are not established by the reviewed sources; omitted from the profile.
- English Heritage notes scholarly disagreement between 604 and perhaps 609 for his death. The profile omits a lifespan instead of using the legacy's unsupported “early 6th century–604.”
- Bede's account of encounters with British bishops serves an ecclesial argument and includes polemical judgments. The profile acknowledges failed cooperation without assigning collective blame or reproducing curse-like narratives.
- Bede emphasizes Roman and Irish contributions while current scholarship identifies stronger British continuity and Frankish aid. The profile deliberately names the wider network.

## Pastoral safety notes

- Mission is invitation to Christ and peaceful witness, never forced conversion, political pressure, manipulation, material leverage, or cultural contempt.
- Gregory's accommodation policy is historically important but does not erase the power imbalance created by royal sponsorship or authorize a modern colonial analogy.
- Æthelberht, Bertha, Liudhard, Frankish clergy, pre-existing British Christians, companions, and later English, Irish, and Roman agents retain their agency.
- Conflict with British church leaders is not used to blame a people, justify domination, or make ecclesial unity depend on silence.
