## Theological review

- Martyrdom is faithful witness under imposed injustice, never a command to seek attacks or remain in preventable danger.
- Pastoral courage includes protection, reporting, escape, shared responsibility, and appropriate help.
- Later descriptions of pagans do not license contempt toward non-Christians or coercive mission.
- Honest source distinctions serve faith rather than weakening it.
- Practices are specific, safe, and non-coercive; the prayer is original and Christ-centered.
