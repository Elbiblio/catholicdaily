## Uncertainties and conflicts

- Birth is approximate. The profile does not calculate an age.
- Later biographies add a pagan-priest father, meeting with Basil, cave-dwelling monastic stereotype, choir claims, and plague-death details. They are not adopted as secure biography.
- Critical attribution separates authentic Syriac writings from a large pseudo-Ephrem corpus. Popular quotations and the traditionally attributed Lenten prayer require their own source labels.
- Poetry communicates through symbol and paradox; it must not be literalized or turned into a detached proof text.
- Anti-Jewish polemic is named as a harmful feature of its historical setting and explicitly rejected as a model for Christian speech.

## Pastoral safety notes

- Jewish people and Judaism must never be treated as a negative foil for Christian identity.
- Ascetic tradition does not authorize unsafe fasting, sleep deprivation, medical neglect, or deliberate exposure to infectious disease.
- Refugees and displaced people are persons with agency and concrete needs, not illustrations for someone else's devotional growth.
- Attribution checks prevent false authority: an appealing saying should not be shared as Ephrem's unless a credible edition supports it.
- Symbolic theology should deepen Scripture-shaped humility, not bypass learning or professional judgment.
