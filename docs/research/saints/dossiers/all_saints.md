# All Saints

## Identity resolution

This collective solemnity includes every person living in heavenly communion with God, both canonized saints and an innumerable company unknown to public history. It is not a biography and therefore has no lifespan. The Roman date is November 1.

## Source ledger

Benedict XVI's 2006 homily controls the unnamed multitude, Christ-centered Beatitudes, imitation, and communion. Francis's 2015 homily independently develops the Beatitudes as the path of discipleship. Benedict XVI's 2010 Angelus controls baptismal holiness and Christian destiny. All were accessed 2026-08-10.

## Claim ledger

The profile does not claim to identify who specifically is saved or to erase the Church's canonization judgments. It states the liturgical belief that the blessed include many not named in the calendar and that Jesus is the primary fulfillment of the Beatitudes.

## Copyright and media decision

All narrative, applications, and prayer are original. No source quotation or image is included. The Revelation multitude is described without reproducing extended biblical text.

## Content review

Review checked November 1, collective scope, every-nation imagery, Beatitudes, communion, and the universal call to holiness. The guide actively counters celebrity logic and vague perfectionism.

## Theological review

Holiness is God's gift in Christ and a real human response, not self-salvation. Saints are honored as fruits of grace and companions, never as rivals to Christ.
