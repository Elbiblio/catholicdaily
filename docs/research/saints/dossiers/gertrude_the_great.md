## Content review

- Used “nun of Helfta” and recorded the community's exact institutional status.
- Centered women's intellectual, scribal, editorial, and intergenerational agency.
- Distinguished Book II's voice from the composite Legatus and noted the missing autograph.
- Treated visions and Sacred Heart language as private revelation and reception, not binding proof.
- Grounded Sacred Heart reception in Dilexit Nos and the nonbinding limit of private revelation in Catechism 66–67.
- Rejected magical promises, coercive spiritual direction, retrospective diagnosis, self-harm, body shame, unsafe fasting, sleep deprivation, and healthcare refusal.
- Confirmed the summary contains 100–150 words.

## Theological review

- Private revelation cannot add to public Revelation or override conscience and safeguarding.
- Contemplation is measured by Christlike love, freedom, care, and truthful community.
