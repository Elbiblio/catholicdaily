# Saint Hilary of Poitiers

## Identity resolution

- Stable ID: `hilary_of_poitiers`
- Profile kind: Individual
- Celebration IDs: `hilary_of_poitiers`, `saint_hilary_bishop_and_doctor_of_the_church`
- Canonical name and aliases: Saint Hilary of Poitiers; Hilarius Pictaviensis
- Feast date and calendar scope: January 13; optional memorial in the General Roman Calendar
- Identity conflicts resolved: The profile identifies the fourth-century bishop of Poitiers and Doctor of the Church, not Pope Hilary. Ancient evidence leaves much of his early life uncertain; the profile therefore uses `c. 310-367`, omits unsupported family detail, and distinguishes the documented exile and writings from later biographical embellishment.

## Copyright and media decision

No translated passage from Hilary, papal catechesis, or encyclopedia prose is reproduced. All app prose and prayer are newly written. No image is included because file-level licensing and attribution were not established.

## Content review

The content review verified the Poitiers identity, approximate dates, adult Baptism, episcopal election, Synod of Béziers, exile, return, major works, scriptural method, January 13 memorial, and 1851 Doctor title. It specifically checked that On the Trinity was begun before exile and substantially developed and completed during exile. It omits uncertain domestic detail and avoids describing fourth-century doctrinal opponents with careless modern labels. Each practice grows from Hilary's particular combination of biblical study, precise speech, endurance, and reconciliation.

## Theological review

The guide clearly confesses Jesus Christ as true God and true man while using language accessible to readers. It does not reduce Trinitarian faith to argument, glorify exile, or equate harshness with orthodoxy. Hilary's witness is presented as baptismal fidelity expressed through Scripture, pastoral responsibility, doctrinal precision, and patient communion. The prayer addresses the Father through Christ and asks the Holy Spirit for faithful speech.
