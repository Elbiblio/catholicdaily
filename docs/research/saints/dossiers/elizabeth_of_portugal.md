## Theological review

- Mercy is concrete stewardship rather than royal sentiment, and peacemaking remains joined to truth and justice.
- Marriage never requires remaining in abuse; family loyalty never cancels boundaries, safeguarding, or appropriate authority.
- Mediation is recommended only when supported and safe, not as unprotected entry into violence.
- The practices preserve dependants, health, consent, and professional support; the prayer is original and Christ-centered.
