# Our Lady, Queen and Patroness of Nigeria

## Identity resolution

This is a Marian title and Nigerian solemnity, not a separate apparition or human biography. Nigerian diocesan sources consistently place the celebration on October 1 and connect it with the bishops' dedication of the newly independent country to Mary in 1960.

## Source ledger

The Catholic Diocese of Umuahia controls the 1960 dedication, local solemnity, date, and peace-justice context. Archbishop Ignatius Kaigama's national Marian address controls episcopal interpretation and Marian social virtues. The Catholic Diocese of Warri independently confirms title, patronage, and October 1. Sources were accessed 2026-08-10.

## Claim ledger

The profile carefully says Nigerian Catholic sources record the bishops' dedication; it does not invent a specific decree text or venue. It distinguishes Nigerian observance from the universal October 1 memorial of Saint Therese and avoids promising automatic political protection.

## Theological review

Mary's queenship depends entirely on Christ and leads to obedience to him. Intercession is directed to God; devotion must become justice, peace, honest citizenship, and service.
