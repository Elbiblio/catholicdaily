- Rejected the Raymond Diocrès corpse story, star dream, supernatural signs, and mottos as biography.
- Preserved six companions, friendship, shared worship, work, and accountability.
- Bounded silence against abandonment of dependants, concealment of abuse, coercive enclosure, food or sleep deprivation, and untreated mental or physical illness.
- Confirmed the summary contains 100–150 words and original prose.

## Theological review

- Contemplation is ordered to Christ, love, and responsibility rather than superiority or escape.
- Solitude never excuses abandonment, coercion, self-neglect, or concealment of harm.
