# Saint Jerome Emiliani

## Identity resolution

- Stable ID: `jerome_emiliani`
- Profile kind: individual
- Celebration IDs: `jerome_emiliani`, `saint_jerome_emiliani`
- Canonical name and aliases: Saint Jerome Emiliani; Girolamo Emiliani; Gerolamo Miani
- Feast date and calendar scope: February 8; optional memorial in the General Roman Calendar
- Identity conflicts resolved: The Italian surname forms Emiliani and Miani both occur in the order's history. The official calendar ID and established English name remain Jerome Emiliani. The reviewed sources support 1486-1537, imprisonment in 1511, a gradual conversion, service to orphaned and vulnerable children, and the Somascan foundation. They do not support romanticizing imprisonment as necessary or good.

## Copyright and media decision

The profile uses original prose and does not reproduce prayers, founder quotations, or source paragraphs. The imprisonment narrative is compressed and non-sensational. No image is included because complete file-specific authorship and licensing were not researched.

## Content review

The factual pass checked Venice and Somasca, the 1486-1537 span, the 1511 captivity, the vow and ecclesial interpretation of release, the later course of conversion, family guardianship, famine relief, care and education of orphaned children, formation of collaborators, later Somascan development, canonization, and formal patronage. It removed unsupported ordination claims and any suggestion that suffering itself created holiness. Current action language was reviewed for safeguarding, consent, confidentiality, screening, training, qualified supervision, and professional boundaries.

## Theological review

Conversion is presented as grace received and embodied in responsibility, not as a reward purchased by trauma. Mary's intercession remains subordinate to Christ, and the founder's care participates in Jesus' promise not to leave his disciples orphaned. The profile refuses saviorism: charity seeks the whole person's dignity and agency, works collaboratively, and accepts accountability.
