## Marian identity safeguard

## Colonial and interreligious safeguard

The basilica's construction belongs to French colonial Algeria. The local Church's own history names armed conquest and settler colonization, while independent architectural scholarship documents assimilationist conversion projects and cultural appropriation. The guide therefore refuses devotion-as-domination, speaks of Algerian history rather than French possession, and centers African agency. Its present-facing practice respects Muslim neighbors and expressly forbids conditioning aid on conversion.
