## Copyright and media decision

Original synthesis and prayer. Modern Holy See, journal, edited translation, and Oxford material is paraphrased. No torture image or portrait was accepted; no file-level rights were verified.

## Content review

All sixteen remain subjects rather than Lawrence's background. The profile labels the primary relation's mediation, gives colonial and Japanese context, avoids graphic torture, preserves an unresolved accusation, and refuses danger-seeking and coerced-apostasy shame. Summary is 100–150 words.

## Theological review

Martyrdom condemns coercion; it does not demand exposure, return to danger, or abandonment of dependants. People may flee, seek asylum, use lawful protection, and need trauma care. Japanese people and religions bear no collective blame.
