# Saint Vincent, Deacon and Martyr

## Identity resolution

- Stable ID: `vincent_of_saragossa`
- Profile kind: Individual
- Celebration IDs: `vincent_of_saragossa`, `saint_vincent_deacon_and_martyr`
- Canonical name and aliases: Saint Vincent, Deacon and Martyr; Vincent of Saragossa; Vincent of Zaragoza
- Feast date and calendar scope: January 22; optional memorial in the General Roman Calendar, with particular local devotion in Valencia
- Identity conflicts resolved: The profile identifies the early-fourth-century deacon of Saragossa martyred at Valencia. The legacy `300-304` lifespan was an invalid reconstruction and is replaced by `d. c. 304`; L'Osservatore Romano allows a probable 304-306 range. Huesca, Valencia, and Saragossa appear as competing birthplace traditions, so no birthplace is asserted. Later speeches, ordeals, prison wonders, and corpse miracles are not narrated as documented events.

## Copyright and media decision

No ancient speech, Prudentius translation, liturgical collect, or modern source paragraph is reproduced. All biography, reflection, practices, and prayer are original. No image is included because file-specific creator, source-page license, credit line, and derivative status were not established.

## Content review

The factual review retained only the common deacon, Saragossa, Valencia, Diocletian-persecution, approximate death, January 22, and early-cult core. It distinguishes reliable early tradition from poetic and later legendary expansion, removes the nonsensical four-year legacy lifespan, and avoids graphic detail. Formal patronage is limited to Valencia and its archdiocese and Lisbon, directly supported by official sources. Every life section and virtue has valid source references.

## Theological review

The guide treats martyrdom as fidelity to Christ under evil coercion, never as approval of torture, execution, or reckless exposure. Courage is attributed to the Holy Spirit rather than personal toughness. The prayer is addressed to Christ, asks protection for those under pressure, and explicitly joins devotion to historical honesty. Practical support emphasizes safety and accompaniment rather than spectacle.
