## Copyright and media decision

All public prose is original synthesis. Modern scholarly prose is paraphrased and the early printed work is used as a textual witness. Freiburg page images and any portraits require separate file-level rights review; no image ships.

## Content review

- Preserved Dominican schools, Paris, Cologne, Thomas, provincial and episcopal service, natural philosophy, and later cult.
- Credited Greek, Arabic, Jewish, and Latin intellectual transmission without triumphalist ownership.
- Distinguished medieval scientia and observation from a modern experimental scientist myth.
- Separated authentic alchemical discussion from pseudo-Albert magic and alchemy.
- Used the Editio Coloniensis as the corpus-authentication control and treated the 1495 print only as a textual witness.
- Named crusade preaching without sanctifying war or anti-Muslim rhetoric.
- Rejected unsafe medical inference and confirmed the summary contains 100–150 words.

## Theological review
