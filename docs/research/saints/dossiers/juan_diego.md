## Copyright and media decision

All public prose is original synthesis. No tilma, manuscript, portrait, or shrine image ships because file-level rights and derivative permissions were not established.

## Content review

- Preserved exact aliases and Q335539; omitted computed age, unsupported patronage, symbols, cause of death, and name etymology.
- Framed the 1531 account as received private-revelation tradition and the 1649 printing as the first securely dated Nahuatl apparition account.
- Rejected conquest triumphalism, coercion, racial essentialism, inflated conversion totals, miracle guarantees, and nationalist ownership.
- Named Indigenous interpretation, languages, communities, and agency without treating Nahua peoples as a single timeless worldview.

## Theological review

- Christ's definitive Revelation remains the measure of devotion; private revelation is never public proof or compelled belief.
- Humility and witness require listening across power differences, not using Juan Diego to sanctify colonial domination.
