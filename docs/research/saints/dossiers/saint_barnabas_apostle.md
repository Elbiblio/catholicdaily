## Uncertainties and conflicts

- Exact lifespan and age are unsupported and omitted.
- “Apostle” is scriptural but does not mean one of the Twelve.
- Acts and Galatians provide different windows on Antioch. Both are retained: Barnabas's genuine leadership and his correctable failure.
- Acts reports a sharp disagreement with Paul but neither a villain nor a later reconciliation. Safe separation can remain morally serious without being spiritual failure.
- Cyprus martyrdom, relic discovery, and expanded biography are later hagiographic reception.
- The Epistle of Barnabas is anonymous. Other authorship conjectures do not become facts.
- Anti-Jewish generalization is forbidden: the inclusion crisis was an intra-Christian dispute involving Jewish followers of Jesus.

## Pastoral safety notes

- Welcoming a formerly violent or accused person must remain evidence-aware, communal, and safeguarding-led; informal trust never overrides protection.
- Generosity must account for dependants, debt, consent, and basic needs. No one should be shamed into giving beyond their means.
- Mission is witness and service, never manipulation, cultural contempt, or coercion.
- Correction should name exclusion without weaponizing shame; canonized people are not beyond accountability.
- Reconciliation cannot be forced where abuse, danger, or unresolved responsibility requires distance.
- Martyrdom traditions do not tell anyone to seek danger or refuse lawful safety.
