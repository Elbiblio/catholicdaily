## Theological review

- The Incarnation and Scripture remain central; eloquence serves mercy, truth, conversion, and human dignity.
- Doctor status does not canonize every ancient formulation, political entanglement, or inherited prejudice.
- Jewish people are not collectively guilty, rejected, or accursed; hatred, persecution, and antisemitism are rejected.
- Doctrinal dispute requires evidence, patient procedure, and freedom from state coercion or mob pressure.
- Practices avoid stereotyping, domination, and performative learning; the prayer is original and Christ-centered.
