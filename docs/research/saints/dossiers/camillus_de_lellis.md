## Theological review

- Conversion supports treatment, recovery communities, financial safeguards, and practical repair rather than shame or magical cure.
- Spiritual care accompanies but never replaces medicine, consent, clinical skill, infection control, safeguarding, or reporting.
- Caregivers need training, teamwork, supervision, rest, and safe workload; exhaustion is not a measure of holiness.
- Patients retain privacy, agency, and the right to accept or decline spiritual involvement.
- Practices offer concrete whole-person care without reckless exposure; the prayer is original and Christ-centered.
