## Uncertainties and conflicts

- The current English Vatican biography gives 19 May 1920 for priestly ordination, while the later formal liturgical decree gives 29 May and explicitly grounds the May 29 memorial in that anniversary. The decree controls.
- Vatican II was collective episcopal work convoked by John XXIII and continued under Paul VI. The profile credits Paul VI's real leadership without treating him as sole author.
- Reception of liturgical reform and other papal decisions remains contested. The profile describes primary aims and implementation mechanisms without declaring every later local decision correct or every criticism disloyal.
- Historical analysis of Paul VI is ongoing. A devotional profile cannot settle all debates and therefore makes its canonization boundary explicit.
