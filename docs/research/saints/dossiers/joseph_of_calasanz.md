## Content review
The profile preserves the educational charism while stating the archival safeguarding finding directly. It rejects secrecy, reputation-first decisions, quiet transfer, promotion, internal-only handling, forced forgiveness, victim blame, and the use of sainthood to excuse abuse.

## Theological review
Modern practice requires immediate safety, lawful reporting, independent investigation, survivor support, record preservation, fair process, and accountable governance. Education and canonization never erase harm or override a child's dignity.
