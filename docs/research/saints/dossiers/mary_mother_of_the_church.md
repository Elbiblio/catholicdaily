## Theological review

- Mary remains Mother of Christ, within the Church, and under Christ's unique mediation; the title neither replaces God nor makes Mary biologically mother of individual believers.
- Maternal language describes faith, charity, prayer, nurture, and ecclesial responsibility rather than a universal role imposed on women.
- No ecclesial metaphor can conceal abuse, silence disclosure, or protect an institution ahead of harmed people.
