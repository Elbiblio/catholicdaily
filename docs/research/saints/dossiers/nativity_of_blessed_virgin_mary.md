## Copyright and media decision
Original synthesis and prayer. Scripture, Vatican material, and Oxford scholarship are paraphrased. The ancient apocryphon is used through a credited translation only to document reception. No image was accepted; infant-Mary art is not evidence for appearance, birthplace, or childhood events.

## Content review
The profile is feast-centered and distinguishes canonical silence from apocryphal reception every time detailed childhood tradition is used. It omits exact birth details, childhood speech, temple scenes as fact, and conception arithmetic.

## Theological review
The feast is centered on Christ, Israel, human dignity, and a free response. It does not blame infertility, guarantee pregnancy, prescribe one family form, shame bodies, or replace qualified reproductive, prenatal, or grief care with devotion.
