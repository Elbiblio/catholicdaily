## Copyright and media decision
Original synthesis and prayer. Scripture, Holy See material, and Austrian Academy history are paraphrased. No image was accepted; battle art, weapons, standards, crowns, and devotional emblems are neither required nor evidence of divine military favor.

## Content review
The profile is observance-centered, names the Spanish and Vienna reception, and explicitly rejects magical invocation, guaranteed outcomes, nationalism, conquest, anti-Muslim hostility, and civilizational enemy language.

## Theological review
Mary remains a Jewish disciple and human intercessor whose name points to Christ, never a deity or independent power. Prayer does not prove that God favors a nation or army and must lead toward dignity, verified relief, reconciliation, and peace.
