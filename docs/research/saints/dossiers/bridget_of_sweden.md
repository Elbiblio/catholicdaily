## Theological review

- Christ is the fullness of public Revelation; no private revelation completes, corrects, or outranks him.
- Canonization recognizes holiness without authenticating every reported message or political instruction.
- Strong impressions remain subject to Scripture, Church teaching, ordinary evidence, accountable counsel, and appropriate healthcare.
- Marriage, parenting, widowhood, travel, and service require consent, safety, health, finances, and care for dependants.
- Practices reject self-harm, punitive fasting, sleep deprivation, inaccessible pilgrimage, and unreviewable certainty; the prayer is original and Christ-centered.
