## Theological review

- Mary is honored within the mystery of Christ and the Church and directs disciples to Christ rather than replacing his unique saving mediation.
- The scapular is an optional, prepared ecclesial sign related to Baptism; it is never a magical contract, talisman, or guaranteed escape from judgment or purgatory.
- Devotion cannot replace conversion, Scripture, the sacraments, ordinary duties, competent medicine, safeguarding, reporting, or justice.
- Practices reject fear, commercial pressure, compulsory enrollment, and unsafe asceticism; the prayer is original, Trinitarian in orientation, and Christ-centered.
