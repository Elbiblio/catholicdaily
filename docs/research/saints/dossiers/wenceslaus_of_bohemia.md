## Copyright and media decision

Original synthesis and prayer. Academic and Vatican prose is paraphrased. Kalashnikova is CC BY 4.0 but still attributed and not copied. No crown, eagle, armor, relic, or national image was accepted without exact object-level rights and contextual review.

## Content review

The profile corrects “king,” preserves the death-date issue, distinguishes political history from hagiography and cult, and names nationalist appropriation. It avoids invented dialogue, family villainy, miracle claims, coercion, and unsafe reconciliation. Summary is 100–150 words.

## Theological review

Authority is evaluated by service and accountability, not dynastic legitimacy. Patronage cannot exclude minorities or nonbelievers. Refusing revenge does not require contact with violent kin, surrender of boundaries, or abandonment of lawful accountability.
