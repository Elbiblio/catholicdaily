## Theological review

- Canonization receives Henry's sanctity without making empire, violence, alliance, or every policy morally normative.
- Religious patronage and reform need transparency, consent, due process, and checks on concentrated authority.
- Childlessness is never blameworthy; later continence traditions cannot justify unilateral marital decisions.
- Cunigunde's agency is named rather than absorbed into a male leadership story.
- Practices direct readers toward accountable shared authority, not coercion or unsafe ordeals; the prayer is original and Christ-centered.
