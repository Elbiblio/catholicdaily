## Copyright and media decision

Original synthesis and prayer. Archaeological, Catholic-reference, historical, and formal prose is paraphrased. No image or relic photograph was accepted; page availability does not grant reuse rights.

## Content review

The profile separates sparse biography, early cult and archaeology, later Passions, relic history, and scientific interpretation. It rejects graphic spectacle, proof claims, omens, and medical or public-safety substitution. Summary is 100–150 words.

## Theological review

Martyrdom is witness, not danger-seeking. Devotion may sustain hope but cannot establish a scientific mechanism, predict disaster, guarantee protection, or replace healthcare, evacuation, emergency services, and responsible inquiry.
