## Mysticism, health, and obedience safeguards

- Visions, ecstasies, and stigmata are reported and recorded claims. The profile does not assert a mechanism, require belief in private revelation, or retrospectively diagnose Mary.
- Sister-scribes, dictated or possibly unsent letters, editorial stages, and the collaboratively shaped 1609 biography remain visible. A critical edition is necessary before direct attribution.
- Severe fasting and mortification are historical reports, never an ascetic regimen to reproduce. Fasting must be proportionate, nutritionally and medically safe, and compatible with duties.
- Illness is not proof of sanctity. Prayer never replaces medical or mental-health evaluation, medication, nutrition, emergency care, or other appropriate treatment.
- Obedience never overrides conscience, bodily safety, safeguarding, lawful reporting, or refusal of abuse. An unsafe instruction requires help, not compliance.
