## Uncertainties and conflicts

- Birth year is approximate and no age is calculated.
- Contemporary and near-contemporary authors shaped Paulinus into an ascetic exemplar; his own letters also involve self-presentation.
- Property renunciation was progressive and managed, not an instant theatrical disposal of all assets.
- The death of Celsus is documented but receives no claim that God caused it to force a vocation.
- Gregory's Vandal captivity story was written more than a century later and is chronologically impossible as stated; no African captivity is asserted.
- Later honor must not erase Therasia's consent, property interests, grief, or contribution.

## Pastoral safety notes

- Major financial or ascetical changes require free spousal agreement and protection of dependants, debts, workers, housing, health, and basic needs.
- Grief should not be interpreted for another person or used to rush irreversible decisions.
- Chastity within a marriage cannot be presumed or imposed from this example.
- Generosity is planned, transparent, and sustainable; poverty is not romanticized.
- No one should enter captivity, trafficking risk, or uncontrolled danger to imitate a late legend; use competent aid and safeguarding routes.
