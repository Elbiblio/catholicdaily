## Copyright and media decision

All public prose is original synthesis. No manuscript, icon, relic, shrine, or martyrdom image ships because file-level rights and derivative permissions were not established.

## Content review

- Preserved exact aliases, Q161775, biblical kind, vocation, date, rank, and color; omitted lifespan, computed age, and unsupported arrays.
- Attributed every event to Acts and treated the speech as literary composition rather than transcript.
- Located every party within a Jewish world and rejected collective blame, anti-Judaism, and anti-Temple flattening.
- Named lethal violence without gore and rejected martyr-seeking, unsafe forgiveness demands, relic certainty, and cure promises.

## Theological review

- Martyr witness honors fidelity to Christ but never makes preventable suffering or public injury desirable.
- Forgiveness does not erase accountability, compel reconciliation, or require unsafe proximity.
