- Feast date and calendar scope: March 23; optional memorial in the General Roman Calendar
- Identity conflicts resolved: The Real Academia de la Historia gives Mayorga de Campos and November 16, 1538, while other official biographies give November 18 and the historic Mayorga-Villaquejida dispute persists. ## Colonial-history and safeguarding review

- The narrative names Turibius as a Spanish crown nominee, former inquisitor, and leader within the Royal Patronage system.
- Defense of Indigenous dignity, language work, and challenges to particular abuses are retained without claiming that he opposed Spanish sovereignty, ended exploitation, or “civilized” Indigenous communities.
- Catechetical translation is presented both as communication across language and as an instrument of conversion and colonial social discipline.
- Visitations are presented as direct pastoral presence and as administrative, fiscal, judicial, and sometimes coercive mechanisms.
- Politically interested complaints and later attributions are not treated as certain facts; severe archival gaps constrain totals and scale.
- Modern practice compensates community expertise, protects dissent and reporting, prevents retaliation, and avoids speaking over people affected by institutional decisions.

## Theological review

Christ's servant leadership judges the use of Church office. Evangelization is not identified with domination, forced conformity, cultural erasure, or imperial success. Human dignity includes Indigenous agency, language, memory, and the right to name harm. Holiness can include courageous reform without making a saint's methods or institutions immune from moral scrutiny, repentance, and correction.
