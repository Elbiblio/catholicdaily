# Saint Angela Merici

## Identity resolution

- Stable ID: `angela_merici`
- Profile kind: Individual
- Celebration IDs: `angela_merici`, `saint_angela_merici_virgin`
- Canonical name and aliases: Saint Angela Merici; Angela of Brescia
- Feast date and calendar scope: January 27; optional memorial in the General Roman Calendar and feast in the city of Brescia
- Identity conflicts resolved: Treccani and most modern controls place her birth around 1474, while the Diocese of Brescia page gives 1470; the profile uses `c. 1474-1540`. The original 1535 Company of Saint Ursula is distinguished from later monastic Ursuline orders and school institutions. The profile does not call Angela a modern educator, religious sister, or founder of a contemporary secular institute category during her lifetime.

## Copyright and media decision

No translated sentence from Angela's Rule, Counsels, Testament, diocesan office, or modern history page is reproduced. The profile uses original synthesis, practices, questions, and prayer. No image is included because file-level licensing and creator attribution were not established.

## Content review

The factual review checked the birth-year conflict, 1535 foundation, Company structure, approvals, differentiated writings, January 27 death and feast, canonization, local patronage, and later Ursuline developments. It deliberately describes the first members as consecrated women in ordinary settings and avoids projecting later monastic, school, and canonical forms backward. It neither romanticizes the constraints on women nor claims modern categories Angela did not use.

## Theological review

The guide centers Angela's consecration and formation on abiding in Christ. Authority respects freedom without abandoning responsibility, and service supports another person's vocation rather than controlling it. The action practice is concrete and agency-preserving. The prayer addresses Christ, asks freedom and wisdom for women and formators, and joins spiritual formation to homes, workplaces, and parishes.
