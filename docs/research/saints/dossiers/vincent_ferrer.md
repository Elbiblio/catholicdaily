## Anti-Judaism, coercion, apocalypticism, and wonder review

- The profile names anti-Jewish discourse, compulsory sermon attendance, segregation, economic and social restriction, and conversion pressure without treating Jewish people as scenery for Vincent's moral growth.
- It distinguishes Christian hope in Christ's return from Vincent's shifting Antichrist dates and refuses to model timetable preaching, fear, or claims of unquestionable private commission.
- It treats popular response as evidence of influence, not proof of every wonder, conversion total, or hagiographic episode.
- It does not transfer medieval contempt toward Jews or Muslims into prayer, practice, or modern mission. The right to question, decline, and retain dignity is explicit.

## Theological review

Christ, not the preacher's charisma or certainty, is the center. Moral exhortation is ordered to prayer, love, truth, and free conscience. The profile explicitly rejects forced conversion, anti-Jewish contempt, legal pressure, fear-based eschatology, and the claim that a religious outcome excuses harmful means. Veneration is joined to repentance, accountability, and solidarity with communities harmed by Christian coercion.
