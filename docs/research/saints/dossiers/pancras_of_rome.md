## Child-safety and martyr safeguard

Young believers have real faith and agency, but adults owe protection. The profile never asks minors to seek danger, keep harmful secrets, confront an abuser alone, refuse care, or prove holiness by fearlessness. Practice directs disclosures to safe adults and qualified safeguarding routes, without amateur investigation or promises of secrecy.

## Copyright and media decision

Exact age, Phrygian origin, uncle, emperor dialogue, fixed persecution, death mechanics, formal patronage, and symbols are not asserted as facts. Modern scholarship and tertiary prose are paraphrased; no liturgical text or Passion translation is reproduced. No image is included without verified reusable licensing.

## Theological review

The gentle Christ receives the burdened. Martyr memory supports truthful courage and adult responsibility, never fascination with a child's death or spiritual pressure on the young. Protection, reporting, treatment, and supported help are compatible with faith.
