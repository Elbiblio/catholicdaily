## Observance, child-safety, and discernment safeguard

- The page explains what the Church commemorates; it never treats “Our Lady of Fatima” as a second Mary with a new life story.
- The three witnesses were children. No child is assigned responsibility for preventing catastrophe, keeping spiritual secrets, performing severe penance, or carrying the world's guilt.
- Approval of private revelation permits prudent devotion but never completes Revelation, creates new doctrine, or obliges assent like the Gospel.
- Solar-event claims never justify staring at the sun. Medical, scientific, or geopolitical claims are not inferred from devotion.
- Reparation means conversion, sacramental life, mercy, justice, and repairing harm—not self-injury, compulsive ritual, bargaining, or scrupulosity.
- Russia and any nationality are never treated as timeless enemy peoples. Prayer for peace leads to truthful, nonviolent action and aid without discrimination.

## Copyright and media decision

The profile rejects a Marian lifespan, deterministic prediction, physical-sun claims, universal eyewitness agreement, scientific proof, secret deadlines, guaranteed war outcomes, claims that consecration mechanically caused political collapse, anti-Russian hostility, medical cures, and unsupported patronage or symbols. Vatican, shrine, liturgical, and scholarly prose is paraphrased; apparition dialogue, secret texts, papal prayers, and modern translations are not reproduced. No image is included without file-specific licensing.

## Theological review

Christ's definitive Revelation governs the observance. Mary directs disciples to hear and keep God's word. Conversion, Rosary prayer, reparation, and peace are interpreted through mercy, justice, sacramental life, and responsible action. Fear, compulsion, self-harm, coercion, child burden, and geopolitical hatred are explicitly rejected.
