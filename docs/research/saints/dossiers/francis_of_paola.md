# Saint Francis of Paola, Hermit

## Identity resolution

- Stable ID: `francis_of_paola`
- Profile kind: individual
- Celebration IDs: `francis_of_paola`, `saint_francis_of_paola_hermit`
- Canonical name and aliases: Saint Francis of Paola, Hermit; Francesco di Paola
- Feast date and calendar scope: April 2; optional memorial in the General Roman Calendar
- Identity conflicts resolved: Francis lived 1416-1507, began as a Calabrian hermit, founded the distinct Order of Minims, and died in France on April 2. An isolated Holy See maritime-directory typo gives 1517, but the 2000 papal address, Treccani, and the broader source record agree on 1507. Royal diplomacy is stated as counsel and participation, not sole authorship of treaties. Miracle stories are classified as later hagiographic tradition.

## Asceticism and health review

- Severe abstinence is described as the vowed corporate discipline of Minim religious, not a universal Catholic obligation, wellness program, or proof of holiness.
- The Rule's own physician-and-superior exception for illness and relief for weakness appears beside the discipline, not hidden in a note.
- General practice uses non-food restraint. The action requires current Church norms, competent spiritual guidance, and clinical advice when health may be affected.
- Minors and people with illness, pregnancy, nursing, frailty, or eating-disorder risk are explicitly told not to endanger health.
- No miracle cure, royal access, or heroic austerity is presented as a substitute for medical care, ordinary duty, or communal accountability.

## Theological review

Christ's call to become servant of all governs the profile. Penance is ordered to conversion and charity, not bodily harm or self-display. Humility includes accepting responsibility and correction. Reconciliation names harm and respects safety rather than forcing contact or premature forgiveness. The prayer asks Christ for grace and treats Francis as intercessor.
