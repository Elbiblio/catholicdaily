## Theological review

- The attacker alone bears guilt for sexual violence; rape cannot make a victim sinful, impure, responsible, or less holy.
- Resistance is not a spiritual test: surviving, freezing, complying under coercion, escaping, or fighting back does not determine dignity.
- Forgiveness is never owed on demand and never cancels reporting, investigation, prosecution, restitution, boundaries, treatment, or permanent loss of access.
- Practices direct adults toward safeguarding pathways and qualified help without asking a child or survivor to disclose publicly; the prayer is original and Christ-centered.
