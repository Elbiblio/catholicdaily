## Uncertainties and conflicts

- Peter Damian's age 120 implies an implausibly early birth and is rejected; modern chronology is approximate and no age is calculated.
- The Vita is near-contemporary but a literary and theological model of reform, not a neutral transcript.
- The exact Camaldoli foundation year 1012 rests on later testimony; the institutional historical synthesis favors a later documentary range.
- Later Camaldolese custom, congregation, and legal structure developed after Romuald. He is foundational, not sole founder of a completed modern order.
- The family duel, coercive penance, visions, demons, miracles, incorruption, and relic-murder stories are not used as secure biography or imitation.
- No direct quotation is assigned to a “Brief Rule of Saint Romuald.”

## Pastoral safety notes

- Solitude must be scheduled, bounded, accountable, and compatible with physical and mental health.
- Silence never becomes punishment, disappearance, secrecy around abuse, or neglect of children, elders, partners, work, or other dependants.
- Fasting, sleep loss, exposure, and self-punishment are not prescribed; medical and mental-health care must not be delayed.
- Obedience does not justify confinement, flogging, coercion, or blocked safeguarding reports.
- Family violence never transfers guilt to another family member or requires vicarious penance.
- Hospitality and community feedback test whether contemplation is becoming Christlike love.
