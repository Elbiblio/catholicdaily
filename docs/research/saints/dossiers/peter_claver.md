## Content review
The profile centers kidnapped Africans as people with languages, knowledge, faiths, families, resistance, and leadership. It states material relief alongside slavery, Jesuit slaveholding, colonial Church complicity, paternalism, compromised religious consent, and the limits of heroic reception.

## Theological review
Baptism never substitutes for freedom, bodily care, or informed consent. Aid is unconditional. Trafficking response should be survivor-led, confidential, culturally and linguistically competent, and routed through trained specialist or emergency services without improvised rescue. Prayer accompanies rather than replaces healthcare, legal aid, liberation, anti-racism, and institutional repair.
