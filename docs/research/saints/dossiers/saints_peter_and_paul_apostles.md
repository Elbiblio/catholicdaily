# Saints Peter and Paul, Apostles

## Identity resolution

This group profile joins two distinct first-century apostles in their traditional June 29 Roman celebration. It does not collapse their ministries or imply that they always agreed. Peter's pastoral commission and Paul's mission among the nations are retained together.

## Source ledger

Francis's 2020 homily controls unity across difference; Benedict XVI's 2005 homily controls catholicity, apostolicity, and Roman witness; John Paul II's 1999 homily controls Peter's confession, Paul's mission, and the shared feast. All were accessed 2026-08-10.

## Claim ledger

The New Testament securely records Peter's denial and restoration, Paul's former persecution and conversion, their different missions, and the Antioch dispute. The Roman martyr tradition is described as ancient tradition rather than supplying unsupported execution details.
