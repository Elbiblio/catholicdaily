## Copyright and media decision

All prose is original synthesis. No Nativity, flight, domestic, or family art ships because file-level reuse rights were not established.

## Content review

- Preserved Matthew and Luke as distinct narratives rather than silently harmonizing later domestic scenes.
- Kept group identity without shared lifespan, age, vocation, or person simulation.
- Used social history to bound reconstruction, not to assert exact poverty, labor, or gender roles.
- Made migration solidarity analogical and rejected nuclear-family superiority, coercion, abuse, stigma, fertility pressure, and protection guarantees.

## Theological review

- Incarnation dignifies embodied family life while Mark widens belonging beyond blood and household form.
- Obedience never overrides consent, child protection, escape from abuse, or professional safeguarding.
