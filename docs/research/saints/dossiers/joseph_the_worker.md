# Saint Joseph the Worker

## Identity resolution

The profile concerns the biblical Joseph of Nazareth under the May 1 worker memorial. Because Scripture supplies no reliable birth or death dates, the legacy negative-year range was removed and only a broad first-century setting remains.

## Source ledger

Redemptoris Custos controls Gospel identity and the workshop of Nazareth. Francis's 2020 May 1 homily controls dignity, work, fair pay, and rest. The 2013 general audience controls Joseph's teaching role and the condemnation of enslaving work. All were accessed on 2026-08-10.

## Claim ledger

Matthew supports Joseph as righteous and as carpenter within Jesus' household. The page does not invent speeches, personality traits, or workshop episodes. The link between work and justice is presented as Church teaching, not as a claim that every job or condition is good.

## Copyright and media decision

Source wording is not reproduced. The narrative, practices, reflection questions, and prayer are original. No quotation or image appears in revision 1.

## Content review

Review removed placeholder biography text and an implausibly precise lifespan. It checked that unemployment, exploitation, unsafe conditions, fair pay, and rest are included rather than romanticizing labor.

## Theological review

Joseph's intercession is subordinate to prayer addressed to God. Work is participation in providential care, never an idol or a measure of a person's worth.
