## Copyright and media decision

The public profile is original synthesis. The old MacMahon translation is credited; modern Handl, Vatican, archaeological, and calendar prose is paraphrased. No catacomb image ships without file-level rights.

## Content review

- Attributed every adverse social, financial, doctrinal, and disciplinary claim to the hostile Refutatio.
- Rejected poverty and enslavement as proxies for criminality or moral character.
- Distinguished cemetery administration, Via Aurelia burial, fourth-century martyr reception, and later well story.
- Kept mercy accountable to evidence, restitution, boundaries, and safeguarding.
- Confirmed the summary contains 100–150 words and original prose.

## Theological review

- Repentance and restoration never require victims to conceal harm, waive justice, or resume unsafe contact.
- Martyr memory does not prescribe provocation, danger-seeking, or refusal of lawful protection.
