## Copyright and media decision

All prose and prayer are original. Modern Holy See, Oxford, Brill, and medical-journal material is paraphrased. No image was accepted. Medical instruments and the racialized medieval “black leg” motif require object-level provenance and contextual review and are not profile symbols.

## Content review

The profile preserves the ancient cult while refusing a merged legendary biography, guaranteed cures, medical misinformation, clinician exploitation, graphic martyrdom, or blame for illness. The summary is 100–150 words.

## Theological review

Prayer accompanies competent healthcare. Charity requires fair access, consent, confidentiality, infection control, professional boundaries, and just support for caregivers. Illness is not evidence of sin or deficient faith.
