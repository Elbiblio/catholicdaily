- Feast date and calendar scope: February 3; optional memorial in the General Roman Calendar and memorial in the Swedish Catholic calendar
- Identity conflicts resolved: The profile retains the stable Hamburg ID but uses the historically safer title `Bishop and missionary`. ## Copyright and media decision

No Rimbert translation, calendar text, diocesan phrase, or museum paragraph is copied. The profile's biography, reflections, practices, questions, and prayer are original. No image is included because file-specific creator, license, credit, and derivative permissions were not established.

## Content review

The factual review checked approximate chronology, Corbie and Corvey, the disputed geographic scope and political setting of the 826 effort, Birka, royal and assembly permission, Hamburg's destruction, Bremen, repeated missions, death, feast, and formal patronage. It treats Rimbert as both indispensable near-contemporary testimony and an agenda-shaped hagiography. It records the archiepiscopal/institutional dispute, omits uncertain miracle claims, and refuses to credit Ansgar with the later conversion of whole peoples.

## Theological review

Mission is framed as witness, negotiated presence, local agency, and material mercy, never cultural conquest or coercion. Political entanglement is named rather than baptized as pure. Setbacks remain losses, not divinely good events. The Scripture connection uses the official Swedish memorial reading to judge mission by light, liberation, justice, and service. The prayer asks Christ to purify mission of pride, pressure, and contempt.
