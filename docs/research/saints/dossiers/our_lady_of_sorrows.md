## Copyright and media decision

Original synthesis and prayer. Scripture, Holy See material, Vatican News, and scholarship are paraphrased. No image was accepted; swords, tears, Pietà art, and private-revelation imagery are neither required nor event evidence.

## Content review

The profile is observance-centered, within 100–150 words, and does not quantify Mary's suffering or simulate a biography. It distinguishes canonical anchors from developed Seven Sorrows devotion and joins lament to safe care.

## Theological review

Christ's Paschal mystery remains central. Suffering is not spiritually desirable by itself, and bereaved or traumatized people are not required to hide pain, forgive on demand, remain unsafe, or replace professional care with devotion.
