## Content review

- Preserved exact aliases, Q44015, biblical kind, vocation, date, rank, and color; omitted lifespan, age, celibacy, death manner, and unsupported arrays.
- Kept son of Zebedee, beloved disciple, Gospel witness/final voice, elder, Patmos seer, and Ephesian reception distinct.
- Presented early reception and modern scholarship as evidence and argument, not settled identity proof.
- Rejected anti-Judaism, purity and superiority claims, miracle guarantees, and certainty about authorship or death.

## Theological review

- Apostolic witness serves truth and love; closeness to Jesus never grants superiority or purity over others.
- Johannine conflict must be interpreted inside its Jewish world and never used for collective blame.
