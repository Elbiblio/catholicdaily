## Copyright and media decision

The profile is an original synthesis. USCCB, Holy See, and Cambridge prose is used at claim level and paraphrased; no extended Scripture translation or copyrighted scholarly wording is reproduced. No quote object is included. No image was accepted because no exact object-level license, creator, source page, credit line, and derivative review were completed.

## Content review

- Rechecked the Twelve/Didymus identity, four Johannine scenes, July 3 feast, red color, and live Q43669 mapping.
- Omitted unsupported legacy dates and age; distinguished canonical evidence from Edessan, Indian, apocryphal, martyrdom, relic, and architecture traditions.
- Removed generic legacy patronage and symbols and avoided the flattening label “doubting Thomas.”
- Confirmed the one-minute summary is within 100–150 words and the prose is original.

## Theological review

- The risen Christ, not doubt as an end in itself, is the center of the guide.
- Questions are welcomed without treating all claims as equally evidenced or bypassing Scripture, community, expertise, and discernment.
- Courage is not death-seeking, and faith is not forced certainty, anti-intellectualism, or acceptance of unsafe authority.
- Practices are realistic and non-manipulative; the prayer is original, explicitly Christ-centered, and not presented as liturgical text.
