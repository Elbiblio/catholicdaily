## Copyright and media decision

All prose is original synthesis. No manuscript, shrine, relic, or murder-scene media ships because file-level reuse rights were not established.

## Content review

- Balanced Becket's liberty claims with legal controversy, faction, sanctions, and institutional power.
- Identified Edward Grim correctly and kept early Lives as partisan/hagiographic evidence rather than verbatim transcript.
- Named lethal violence without gore; Henry's rhetoric and climate remain distinct from a proven direct order.
- Treated miracles, relics, and pilgrimage as reception, never cure certainty or martyr-seeking.

## Theological review

- Conscience is joined to accountability, due process, negotiation, and de-escalation.
- Ecclesiastical liberty does not become clerical immunity or blanket supremacy.
