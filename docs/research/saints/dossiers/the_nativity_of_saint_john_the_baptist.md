## Uncertainties and conflicts

- Wikidata Q3870479 is the feast. Person and artwork entities are not substituted.
- The June 24 date is received liturgical chronology, not a claim that a modern birth certificate is recoverable.
- Luke's muteness narrative must not be generalized into disability as punishment or used to stigmatize people who communicate differently.
- The feast does not promise conception, explain infertility, or rank biological parenthood above adoption, fostering, celibacy, or other vocations.
- A child's prophetic calling does not authorize adults to determine a child's future or ignore consent and safeguarding.

## Pastoral safety notes

- Speak about infertility, miscarriage, pregnancy, adoption, fostering, and childlessness without unsolicited explanations, blame, or promises.
- Every child has dignity before achievement and must be protected from spiritualized control or unsafe publicity.
- Adults should support vocation through listening, consent, age-appropriate freedom, and safeguarding—not deterministic predictions.
- Zechariah's narrative never licenses mockery or exclusion of disabled people.
- Community welcome should be practical, consent-based, and attentive to parents' and children's real needs.
