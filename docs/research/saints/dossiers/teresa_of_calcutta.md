## Copyright and media decision

All narrative, practices, and prayer are original. Familiar quotations are omitted because they are unnecessary and often circulate without stable provenance. No image is included in revision 1.

## Content review

Review checked chronology, congregation name and date, direct works of mercy, international growth, death in 1997, and canonization in 2016. It avoids romanticizing poverty or encouraging untrained medical intervention.

## Theological review

Christ is encountered in the poor without identifying poverty itself as holy. Prayer and competent service remain joined; lack of consolation is not treated as abandonment by God.
