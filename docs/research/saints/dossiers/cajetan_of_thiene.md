## Theological review

- Poverty never authorizes neglect of dependants, coercive giving, unsafe financial risk, or shame.
- Violence and displacement remain harms; survival, flight, treatment, and rebuilding are legitimate.
- Financial action requires competence, consent, transparency, and fair dealing.
