## Text, attribution, and learning safeguards

- Bede's Ecclesiastical History is a primary work with stated sources, theological aims, inherited conventions, and early medieval genre limits; it is not modern neutral reportage.
- Bede's own notice and Cuthbert's separate death letter must not be collapsed. This profile relies on the former and does not import the latter's death scene.
- A concise self-account does not justify claims about every journey, relationship, motive, or daily practice. The profile does not say he never traveled.
- Later title stories and unattributed sayings are excluded from the secure biography. Respect for Bede includes correcting a formal modern source's apparent 737 typo to 735.
- Learning is framed as prayerful, truthful, and communal. It does not license status-seeking, plagiarism, source erasure, or certainty beyond the evidence.
